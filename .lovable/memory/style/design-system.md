Primary: muted teal `hsl(160, 45%, 40%)` light / `hsl(160, 40%, 55%)` dark
Background: warm stone `hsl(40, 20%, 95%)` light / `hsl(30, 6%, 7%)` dark
Cards: warm white `hsl(40, 25%, 99%)` — subtle depth, not pure white
Borders: warm gray `hsl(35, 10%, 87%)` — barely visible, not cool/blue
Glass cards: reduced shadow intensity for flatter premium feel
Status badges: desaturated backgrounds (60% opacity light, 15% dark)
Inspired by Cursor's calm, warm, enterprise aesthetic.
