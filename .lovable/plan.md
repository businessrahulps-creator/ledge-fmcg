# Ledge vs Vyapar: a 2-page creative brief for ChatGPT

A single, tight `.md` file you can paste into ChatGPT to make ad creatives. Saved to your Files as `ledge-creative-narrative.md`.

## How I will build it

1. **Scan the Ledge app**: every module (orders, dispatch & bill, GST bills, receipts, credit notes, returns, stock and warehouses, dealers, sales team, schemes, targets, claims, Command reports, roles, WhatsApp sharing, PDFs, Ledge Intelligence). Only claims the app actually delivers go in.
2. **Refresh Vyapar research**: current pricing (the ₹2,500–₹3,000/year per device figure), its real strengths, and what reviewers say it lacks. Each claim gets a source link.
3. Reuse the earlier `ledge-vs-vyapar.md` findings where they still hold.

## What the document contains (about 2 pages)

1. **One-line positioning**: "Vyapar does billing. Ledge runs your distribution business."
2. **Vyapar's real strengths (USPs)**: stated fairly, e.g. cheap, simple, offline billing, strong brand among shopkeepers. Being honest makes the pitch more believable.
3. **What makes Ledge different**: built for distributors (dealers, reps, godowns, schemes, claims, targets), one price for the whole team instead of per device, credit limits the system enforces, stock that moves with dispatch, bills that can't be changed once issued, Ledge Intelligence.
4. **Why choose Ledge**: for a distributor starting fresh.
5. **Why switch to Ledge**: for someone already on Vyapar, with answers to "I already paid" and "my staff know it".
6. **What makes Ledge amazing**: the moments that feel good, e.g. an order to a GST bill in one step, WhatsApp confirmations, live dues.
7. **How to talk about price**: compare the cost for a real team (owner + 2 salesmen + accountant), not the sticker price. Uses the ₹799/month whole-team pricing we worked out earlier, clearly marked as proposed.
8. **Ready-made lines for ChatGPT**: 10 headlines, 5 short hooks, 3 creative ideas, and a tone guide (founder to founder, plain English, Indian FMCG).
