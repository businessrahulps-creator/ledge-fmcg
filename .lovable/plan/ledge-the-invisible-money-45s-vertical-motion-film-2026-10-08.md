# Ledge — "The Invisible Money" (45s vertical motion film)

## What you get
A 45-second vertical video (1080 × 1920, 30 fps) saved to Files. It has a young Indian female voice reading your script word for word, an original light electronic music track, soft click and whoosh sounds, and Bevel-style animated interface cards. No app changes.

## Look (from your mood board)
- **Canvas:** warm ivory and soft grey. Forest green for the brand moments ("Meet Ledge" and the ending).
- **Accent:** electric lime for the glowing dot and anything positive. Soft blue, lavender, coral and pastel orange appear only inside data pieces (rings, dot grids).
- **Cards:** white, softly raised, rounded, with a gentle shadow. Stacked-card depth like the list reference. Dotted connector lines with travelling pulses like the network reference.
- **Type:** one clean modern sans (SF Pro / Helvetica-like discipline) with large, calm headlines and minimal on-screen words.
- **One thread:** the lime dot appears in every scene, so the film reads as one continuous move.

## Beats (timed to the measured voice)
```text
0-5s    ivory dot grid fades in; one lime dot glows, camera drifts in, dot breaks free
5-14s   dot travels through 4 floating cards (dealer not ordered, order waiting,
        payment overdue, stock low), each morphing out of the dot; dotted links pulse
14-18s  cards slide together and fold into the Ledge wordmark; ivory -> forest green
18-25s  wordmark opens into 5 connected modules: Orders, Stock, Payments, Dealers, Team;
        a GST bill card slides out of Orders; camera glides between them
25-32s  "Today's work": Call dealer / Send pending order / Restock product,
        each completes into a green ring tick
32-37s  order card expands: Order confirmed -> GST bill created -> Stock updated,
        flowing into a calm summary card
37-42s  lime dot returns and joins other dots into a rising line;
        LESS CHECKING. / MORE SELLING. / MISS LESS.
42-45s  forest green end card: white Ledge wordmark, "Find what's missing. Grow what's next.",
        getledge.in, "Start your 30-day free trial"; three-note sign-off
```

## Motion
Shape morphs instead of hard cuts, gentle springs with slight overshoot, continuous camera moves, and small UI clicks landing on spoken words. No sample revenue figures or growth claims. The chart has no axis numbers.

## Checks before delivery
- Stills from every scene, checked at full size for spelling, spacing and cropping.
- Voice timing matches each beat, and "missing less" is delivered slower.
- Music sits under the voice, with loudness checked.
- I'll share key frames and the final file.

## Technical details
- **Voice:** Lovable AI speech (Gemini TTS, Indian-English female). The script is sent exactly as written, and beat timings are taken from the measured audio.
- **Video:** Remotion (code-rendered) in /tmp/remotion, built as a separate composition from earlier films. All motion is frame-based.
- **Music and sound effects:** generated in code (synth bass, plucks, light percussion, ~118 BPM) and mixed with FFmpeg. The voice is kept clear above the music.
- **Wordmark:** the real Ledge mark from the project's existing logo asset.
- **Budget:** no image generation needed. At most one re-voice if the timing misses 45 seconds by more than 1.5 seconds.
