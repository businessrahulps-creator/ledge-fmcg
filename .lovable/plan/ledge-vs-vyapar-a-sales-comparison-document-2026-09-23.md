# Ledge vs Vyapar: a sales comparison document

A researched, ready-to-send document you can hand to prospects who currently use Vyapar, answering "why switch to Ledge?".

## What the research shows about Vyapar

- It is a **billing and accounting app for the smallest businesses** — shopkeepers, kirana stores, sole traders. Reviewers place it as "Tally for the smartphone generation".
- Independent reviews say it is explicitly **not for** multi-location businesses, real-time consolidation, multi-warehouse or lot-level stock, or teams growing past ~5 people — with "replatforming will be painful" as the warning.
- Pricing is **per device, per year** (mobile ~₹2,999, desktop ~₹4,999, bundle ~₹6,999), which punishes a business the moment it adds field staff.
- Reviewers repeatedly flag weak inventory depth (no reorder automation, no API), and some long-term users report data-sync glitches and slipping support.

Every claim in the document will be sourced with a link so you can defend it in front of a client.

## The argument the document makes

Vyapar sells **billing**. Ledge runs a **distribution business**. That is the whole pitch, expressed in six reasons:

1. **Built for distribution, not for a shop counter** — dealers, sales reps, routes, godowns, schemes, claims and targets are first-class in Ledge; in Vyapar they don't exist.
2. **A sales team, not a single till** — role-based access (owner, sales manager, accountant, salesperson), per-rep targets and live performance. Ledge prices by business, not by device.
3. **Schemes and claims that settle themselves** — the thing distributors actually lose money on, and the thing a billing app has no concept of.
4. **Money you can trust** — server-side credit limits, canonical balances, immutable GST documents, audited voids and credit notes. Answers the "data mismatch" fear in Vyapar's own reviews.
5. **Stock tied to dispatch** — multi-warehouse, live stock health, automatic deduction on dispatch and restock on return; dispatch is refused when stock isn't there.
6. **Ledge Intelligence — "My Business"** — the headline differentiator: photo-to-order from a handwritten chit, voice order entry, scheme suggestions, plain-language questions about your own business, daily briefings. Nothing in Vyapar's feature list comes close.
